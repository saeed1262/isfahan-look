# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- Four palettes (Dawn, Workday, Evening, Night) that follow sunrise and sunset at your place, starting at your time zone's main city.
- Tile motifs in seven patterns: the band behind titles, the file explorer's lattice, diamond checkboxes, the `[!mihrab]` arch.
- Serif headings (Cormorant Garamond) and a book face for pages (Literata), both bundled.
- Page mode: book typography, margin footnotes, a drop cap, the reading-progress line and quiet chrome.
- The property ribbon, in three styles.
- Raised callouts, pressed-in quotes, table cards, tag and code chips, and a tab bar and ribbon to match.
- A new tab: the time, the date and the phase, the weather and the next 12 hours, search and recent notes.
- A Create a sample page command, and three templates (Book, Long-read and Daily).
